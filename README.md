# Hi, I'm Uri Naor 👋

I'm a product-minded software developer who likes to build solutions that solve real problems.

Currently, I work at the Technion's Faculty of Electrical and Computer Engineering, where I develop the web portal for their High Performance Computing (HPC) cluster, which helps students and researchers train AI models that will empower the future. 

I build with a modern MERN alternative, pairing MongoDB and React.js with Bun and Hono as high performance equivalents to Node.js and Express.js - backed by a strong foundation in C and Java. 

My background isn't purely technical. Before fully pivoting into software engineering, I built and scaled a top-rated Udemy business that shared life-saving first aid skills with over 15,000 students globally. That experience taught me how to find a critical need and build a product around it. I moved into software development to apply that exact same mindset, but at a much larger scale.

When I'm not coding or learning, you can find me hiking in nature, having some laughs over a beer with a friend, or dusting off my trusty PS2 controller for a nostalgic trip.
